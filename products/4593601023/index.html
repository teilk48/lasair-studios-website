Dragon Claw Jesmonite Tea Light Holder Give your home a touch of fantasy with this dragon claw inspired tea light holder. Its textured form and flowing blue, lavender and rust marbling make it a distinctive piece for a gothic display, altar or cosy corner. Each holder is individually cast by hand in Scotland using Jesmonite AC100, a water based composite material. The marbled pattern and handmade surface mean every piece has its own variations. The holder is designed to fit a tea light up to 35 mm in diameter. If you would like a different colour combination or finish, please contact me before ordering to check availability. Finish options Sealed: Finished with a decorative protective sealer. Unsealed: Supplied in its natural cast finish, ready for your own decoration or finishing. Product details Material: Jesmonite AC100 composite Height: Approximately 125 mm Width: Approximately 75 mm Depth: Approximately 95 mm Tea light opening: Suitable for tea lights up to 35 mm in diameter Origin: Handmade in Scotland Please note As each piece is handmade, slight variations in colour, texture and surface finish are natural characteristics of Jesmonite. Jesmonite is not inherently waterproof, so do not leave the holder submerged in water. However Jesmonite is heat resistant and suitable for candle use as well as LED tea lights (ensure diameter fits description) Use only with a metal cup tea light that fits the holder. Place it on a stable, heat resistant surface, keep it away from flammable materials and never leave it unattended while lit. Allow the holder to cool before handling.
