Handmade Jesmonite gothic Bust Add a little gothic character to your home with this miniature gothic bust. Its hooded skull design and marbled finish make it a distinctive accent for a shelf, desk, altar or Halloween display. Each bust is individually cast by hand in Scotland using Jesmonite AC100, a water based composite material. The marbling and handmade finish mean every piece is unique. The red bust shown is an example of the available colours. Please contact me before ordering if you would like a particular colour combination. Finish options Sealed: Finished with a decorative protective sealer. Unsealed: Supplied in its natural cast finish, ready for your own decoration or finishing. Product details Material: Jesmonite AC100 composite Height: 117 mm Width: 52 mm Depth: 44 mm Weight: 215 g Origin: Handmade in Scotland This listing is for one bust. Other colours are available. This is a decorative ornament, not a candle holder. While Jesmonite is heat resistant, this figure is not designed to hold a candle and would require some for of support to prevent accidental tipping of the figure. Jesmonite is not inherently waterproof, so keep the bust dry and do not leave it submerged in water. As each piece is handmade, slight variations in colour, marbling, texture and surface finish are natural. Handcrafted in Scotland by Lasair.
