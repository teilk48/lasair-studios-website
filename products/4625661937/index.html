Highland Cow Wall Art, Layered Wooden Relief Plaque, 25 × 21 cm

From £35.00

A Highland cow peeks into the frame, its shaggy fringe and sweeping horns picked out in layers of real wood. The raised detail catches the light from different angles, giving the piece a sense of depth that comes through especially well on a shelf or mantel. Made in Fife, Scotland, each plaque is built from layers of 3 mm hardwood plywood, laser cut, stained and assembled by hand. The walnut, dark oak and ebony stains give the layers their contrast while leaving the wood grain visible. Natural variations in the grain and finish make each piece slightly different. The finished plaque measures 25 cm high × 21 cm wide. Display it propped on a shelf, mantel or side table, or select the optional sawtooth picture hanger if you’d like to mount it on a wall. The hanger is included only when selected.
