Scottish Wildlife Slate Coasters, Set of 6, Laser Engraved, 10 × 10 cm

£35.00

Bring a little of the Scottish countryside to your table with this set of six laser engraved slate coasters. Each coaster features a different animal, from a shaggy Highland cow peering over a fence to a fox curled up among woodland leaves. The six designs are Highland cow, red deer stag, red squirrel, pine marten, robin and red fox. Each coaster measures approximately 10 × 10 cm and is treated with food safe mineral oil. The pale engraving contrasts with the dark slate, and the natural texture and edges vary slightly from coaster to coaster. Every set comes in a handmade, engraved wooden storage box. Made in Fife, Scotland, the set makes a thoughtful gift for anyone who loves Scottish wildlife and the countryside.
